Google Trends

Do Google searches increase before tick-related vet visits?

This graph compares Queensland Google searches about ticks with veterinary records mentioning ticks. The two lines use different scales: Google search interest (0–100) and number of matched veterinary records.

How to read this: Use the legend to identify the Google search line and the veterinary record line. Google Trends scores range from 0 to 100; 100 means the highest relative search interest in the selected period, not 100 searches. The other line shows records mentioning ticks. If searches rise before records, they might offer an early signal, but our research has not shown a consistent improvement in forecasting from Google Trends. The lines use different vertical scales.

TRANSPARENCY

What should you know about these numbers?

What counts as a tick-related record?

At present, a consultation row is counted when specified tick-related terms appear in its text fields. This classification is preliminary and needs validation against your notebook method.

Are these unique consultations?

Not necessarily. They are source rows, and the source datasets have overlapping coverage. Clinic identifiers also cannot safely be assumed to match across sources.

Does Google Trends predict demand?

This page shows a comparison, not proof of forecasting improvement. Use the notebook's validated out-of-sample evaluation to assess predictive value.
